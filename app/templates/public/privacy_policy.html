What we collect

When you buy a ticket, we collect the name, email address, and (billing) address you provide at checkout, along with which tickets you purchased and how you paid.

Why we collect it

We use this information to fulfil your order: to issue your ticket(s), send you an order confirmation and any invoice by email, and — if required by local tax law — to include on the invoice itself.

How long we keep it

We keep order and buyer information for as long as needed to fulfil your order and to meet our own legal obligations, including statutory accounting/tax retention periods for invoices (commonly several years, depending on jurisdiction). Outside of an issued invoice's retention requirement, we aim to keep no more personal data than necessary.

Requesting deletion

You may ask us to erase the personal details attached to your order. Where an order has an invoice already issued against it, we may need to retain the buyer name/address on that invoice to meet our own accounting/tax obligations, even after erasing it elsewhere; we'll let you know if that applies to your request. To make a request, contact the event organizer directly.
